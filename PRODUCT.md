# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Casual learners and players exploring sailing basics in a browser. On desktop they may experiment with simulator features; on mobile they need a usable-enough, simplified play experience rather than full simulator configuration.

## Product Purpose

A lightweight sailing simulator/trainer that teaches wind angle, no-go zones, tacking, VMG, simple course navigation, and environmental effects through direct play. Success means users can quickly understand how wind affects the craft and complete simple buoy routes without needing sailing jargon first.

## Brand Personality

Playful, instructional, and nautical. The interface should feel like an approachable cockpit/instructor overlay rather than a professional race instrument stack.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense desktop-only control panels on mobile, tiny touch targets, hidden critical controls, and visual clutter that competes with the canvas. Avoid treating mobile as a full simulator cockpit with every advanced control exposed.

## Design Principles

- Teach through motion: keep the water, wind, boat, no-go sector, and buoy visible as the primary learning surface.
- Mobile is simplified play: prioritize rudder, sail, tack/reset, and core feedback over advanced configuration.
- Keep critical controls reachable: touch targets must be large enough and near the thumb zone.
- Progressive complexity: advanced modes and environment controls are available on desktop, but should not dominate small screens.
- Explain failure clearly: stuck, seasick, no-go, and reset states must have explicit next actions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target practical accessibility: readable text, large touch controls on mobile, clear contrast against dark overlays, keyboard shortcuts on desktop, and no essential instruction conveyed by color alone. Respect reduced-motion expectations where animations are introduced.
